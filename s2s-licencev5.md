# Use of data accessed via this service 

The datasets available via this service are **The Sub-seasonal To Seasonal (S2S) dataset**, which consists of ensemble forecasts and reforecasts from participating global numerical weather prediction and research centres. 

Data from following centres are included and made available for online access: 

- **BoM**: Bureau of Meteorology, Melbourne, Australia 
- **CMA**: China Meteorological Administration, Beijing, China 
- **CNR-ISAC**: Istituto di Scienze dell'Atmosfera e del Clima, Bologna, Italy 
- **CNRM**: Le Centre National de Recherches Météorologiques, Toulouse, France 
- **CPTEC**: Centro de Previsao Tempo e Estudos Climaticos, Cachoeira Paulista, Brazil 
- **ECCC**: Environment and Climate Change Canada, Toronto, Canada 
- **ECMWF**: European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts, Reading, Europe 
- **HMCR**: Hydrometeorological Centre of Russia, Moscow, Russia 
- **IAP-CAS**: Institute of Atmospheric Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing, China 
- **JMA**: Japan Meteorological Agency, Tokyo, Japan 
- **KMA**: Korea Meteorological Administration, Seoul, Korea 
- **NCEP**: National Centres for Environmental Prediction, Washington, DC, USA 
- **UKMO**: United Kingdom's Met Office, Exeter, United Kingdom 

New data are added to the archive from contributors on an ongoing basis. 

Different S2S data available via the ECMWF Data Store (ECDS) are provided under two types of a licence depending on the model and the respective data provider, namely they are provided either under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (**CC BY 4.0**) or under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (**CC BY-NC 4.0**). 

Depending on the respective data, registration will provide you with access to the S2S realtime type of forecast with a delay of **48 hours or 1 week** after the initial time of the forecasts, as outlined below: 

- **48 hours** for CMA, CNR-ISAC, CPTEC, ECCC, ECMWF, HMCR, IAP-CAS, JMA, KMA, NCEP data 
- **1 week** BoM, CNRM, UKMO data

The reforecasts can be accessed without any restrictions.
